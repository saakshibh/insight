Clothing Trends: Past & Future Forecast

Past Trends

  • Zara - Known for fast fashion between 2010 to 2020
  • H&M - Sustainable fashion push from 2020 onwards
  • Local boutiques - Spikes during festive seasons & weddings

Predicted Future Trends

Based on fabric popularity, seasonality, and customer mentality:

  • Organic cotton and bamboo fabrics will gain traction (2025-2035)
  • Personalized & AI-designed outfits will trend among Gen Z
  • Season-specific wardrobes will see more online demand (e.g., linen in summer)
